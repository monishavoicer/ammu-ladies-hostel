A place to stay in Anna Nagar, for women

Ammu Ladies Hostel is a girls' hostel with two branches in Anna Nagar, Chennai: Anna Nagar (H Block) and Shanthi Colony (4th Avenue, AF Block). It offers shared-room and paying guest accommodation for college students and working women.

Choose from four, six and eight sharing rooms, with AC and non-AC options. Wi-Fi, home-style food, a study hall and a 24/7 warden are part of the stay. For current rent and availability, please contact the hostel directly.
